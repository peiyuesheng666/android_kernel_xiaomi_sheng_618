# r15 boot acceptance and rejected ambient-transition behavior

r15 was flashed to ODM/vendor/vbmeta on slot b. Reboot completed, boot animation
stopped, and all six installed partition hashes matched the expected chain.
The installed owning-adapter core equals d3908ae8be949614ad63a0b884927bb390df6e9327771a5ae0905c212c0fa303.
Composer PID 1400 stayed running during the inspected snapshots. SurfaceFlinger
reported DEVICE / Device/Device for visible layers and actual 144 Hz DRM timing.
The user confirmed normal picture/touch and visible RGB and Night Light effects.

Ambient adaptation is **not accepted**. The user reported a warm/cool/warm
transition when enabling Night Light and a transient yellow cast on waking and
entering the password screen even with Night Light off. Temporarily setting
display_white_balance_enabled=0 removed both symptoms according to the user.
Its previous saved key was absent. Keep this containment in place while fixing
the feature; do not claim disabling a requested feature is its completed repair.

The retained display dump shows front-CCT sensor/filter histories containing
0 K, while the derived target reached the lower clamp. Zero is not a valid
Kelvin reading; upstream AmbientFilter accepts it. The sensor bridge must reject
invalid samples without altering other sensors, metadata or flush events.

The 35-sample hardware trial with DWB enabled showed B/R initially rising from
0.92871 to 0.93637, then falling, and G/R jumping from 0.84485 to 0.87189 at
about 1.07 seconds. With DWB disabled, the 35 Night Light samples changed
monotonically to the same final G/R=0.74881 and B/R=0.50943. The standard DWB
exit lasts 1 second while Night Light fades over 3 seconds. At its nominal CCT,
the DWB controller replaces its computed Bradford matrix with identity; D65
XYZ and the framework's 6500 K Planckian XYZ are different, leaving an extra
discontinuity at the end. Calibration and timing need separate correction.

Raw evidence and the bounded sampling script are in workspace
reports/device/r15-runtime. No screen pixels, camera activation, biometric
templates, data erase or slot change were used. Existing Dolby/media and CamX
crashes were also observed and remain separate pending hardware issues.
